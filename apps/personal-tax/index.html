05 Self Assessment

Full Compass Personal Tax

Self Assessment for individuals, sole traders and partners, from 2020-21 to 2026-27, each year on its own rules.

Download the suite Next: Pay →

What it does Self Assessment

  1. The return and its pages

    Employment, self-employment, partnerships, property, savings and dividends, foreign income, capital gains, residence and more.

  2. The calculation

    Income tax at UK, Scottish and Welsh rates, Class 2 and Class 4 National Insurance, student loans, the High Income Child Benefit Charge and payments on account.

  3. Figures from Business Tax

    Sole traders' and partners' figures are taken across, with a warning if the accounts change afterwards.

  4. Forms and online returns

    Returns printed on HMRC's own forms, and online returns checked against HMRC's schemas. Online filing is being tested with HMRC's test service.

  5. Checked against HMRC's cases

    Its calculations are checked against HMRC's own Self Assessment test cases.

Works with Takes sole traders' and partners' figures from Business Tax.

Full Compass is a test version: it has not been recognised or approved by HMRC or Companies House, so please don't use it to file real returns or accounts yet.
