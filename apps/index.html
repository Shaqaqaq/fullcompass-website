01 The apps

A start page and five apps

Each app does one part of a practice's work well, and they share one client list and one data file. Choose a room, or walk the gallery.

  1. 01Start pageFull Compass Accountancy SuiteWhere the day starts: the practice's clients across every app, what is due, and each app one click away, opened on the client you are looking at.
  2. 02Accounts productionFull Compass AccountsYear-end accounts from the trial balance, on charts of accounts for limited companies, sole traders, partnerships and farms.
  3. 03Bookkeeping and VATFull Compass BooksBookkeeping and VAT returns for the practice's clients, kept on the client's own Accounts chart, so the year-end trial balance goes straight into the accounts, line for line.
  4. 04Corporation taxFull Compass Business TaxCorporation tax computations and returns, taken from the accounts, with a warning whenever the accounts change after the figures were taken.
  5. 05Self AssessmentFull Compass Personal TaxSelf Assessment for individuals, sole traders and partners, from 2020-21 to 2026-27, each year on its own rules.
  6. 06PayrollFull Compass PayPayroll for the practice's client companies and their directors, with each company linked to its client in the suite.

One setup, the apps you choose
