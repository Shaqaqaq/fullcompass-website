02 Installing

Installing takes a minute or two

  1. Save the setup

    Your browser may say the file isn't commonly downloaded. In Edge, choose the three dots beside the download, then Keep, then Show more, then Keep anyway. In Chrome, choose Keep.

  2. Open it, past Windows' warning

    Until the setup is signed with a publisher's certificate, Windows may show a blue box, "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info, then Run anyway.

  3. Read the notice, choose who it's for

    Just me is the usual choice: it installs for your Windows user, and no administrator is needed. Everyone who uses this computer is for a practice whose staff share one computer; it needs an administrator, who then installs the updates.

  4. Tick the apps you want

    They are all ticked to start with, and the start page always comes. Choose Install, then Finish with "Open Full Compass Accountancy Suite now" ticked.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • About 300 MB of free space.
  • Microsoft Edge WebView2, which most PCs already have. If yours doesn't, the setup downloads it, so be online the first time.

Full Compass is for Windows only for now.

Checking the download

To be sure the file arrived whole, compare its SHA-256 with the one above. In PowerShell:

Get-FileHash "$env:USERPROFILE\Downloads\Full-Compass-Accountancy-Suite-Setup-0.2.7.exe"

03 Updates and your data

Updates come to you

When the start page opens, it looks for updates. An app with a newer version shows Update available and an Update button, and Update all brings the whole suite up to date.

The start page backs up your data file before anything is installed, and checks each download against a signed list of the latest versions. You can turn the check off in Tools > Settings.

To add an app you didn't install at first, use Tools > Install Apps.

Your data stays on your computer

Accounts, Books, Business Tax and Personal Tax share one data file, and Pay keeps its own, both in your own Windows user's folders:

%APPDATA%\uk.fullcompass.suite
%APPDATA%\uk.fullcompass.pay

Nothing is sent anywhere except the update check and what you yourself file with HMRC or Companies House. Read the privacy note.
