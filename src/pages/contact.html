Reporting a problem

It helps to know:

  • which app, and its version (Help > About);
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
  • a screenshot, if it shows the problem.

Please don't send real client, employee or taxpayer details: describe the problem with the demonstration data, or with made-up figures, instead.
