Full Compass Accounts

Year-end accounts from the trial balance, on charts of accounts for limited companies, sole traders, partnerships and farms.

Download the suite Next: Books →

What it does Accounts production

  1. Postings and the trial balance

    Posting entries, journals, opening balances brought forward, the trial balance and the nominal ledger.

  2. Statutory accounts for companies

    Micro-entity (FRS 105), small (FRS 102 Section 1A), dormant, and the filleted copy for Companies House. FRS 102 in full, for larger companies, is being added.

  3. Sole traders and partnerships

    Their accounts, with management reports and lead schedules.

  4. iXBRL and Companies House

    Accounts in iXBRL, and filing at Companies House: now with Companies House's test service, and its live service once Companies House has approved the software.

  5. Every report on A4

    To print, save as PDF, or export to Word or Excel.

Works with Takes the year-end trial balance from Books, and hands its figures on to Business Tax.

Full Compass is a test version: it has not been recognised or approved by HMRC or Companies House, so please don't use it to file real returns or accounts yet.
