What it does Start page

  1. One client record

    A company's, partnership's or sole trader's details are entered once and set up in the apps you tick.

  2. Deadlines for the practice

    Accounts to Companies House, company tax returns and corporation tax, Self Assessment returns and payments, and payroll submissions, each showing how its date is worked out.

  3. A card for each client

    What every app holds for them, with a button to open that app on the client.

  4. Users and sign-in

    Shared by the apps, with each person's apps and clients.

  5. Backups and updates

    Backup and restore of the data file, and updates for every app.

Works with Every other app opens from here, already on the client you chose.

Full Compass is a test version: it has not been recognised or approved by HMRC or Companies House, so please don't use it to file real returns or accounts yet.
