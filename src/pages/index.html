02 The apps

Everything a small practice does, in its own app

A start page and five apps, grouped as the work is: accounts, tax and payroll. Install the ones you need; add the others later.

  • Start page

    Full Compass Accountancy Suite

    Your clients across the apps, the practice's deadlines, and every app one click away.

  • Accounts production

    Full Compass Accounts

    Postings, trial balance and statutory accounts for companies, sole traders and partnerships.

  • Bookkeeping and VAT

    Full Compass Books

    Invoices, bills, banking and VAT returns, kept on the same chart of accounts as the accounts.

  • Corporation tax

    Full Compass Business Tax

    Tax computations and CT600 returns from the accounts, and sole traders' self-employment pages.

  • Self Assessment

    Full Compass Personal Tax

    Self Assessment computations and returns, with sole traders' figures taken from Business Tax.

  • Payroll

    Full Compass Pay

    PAYE, National Insurance, statutory pay, pensions and RTI for the companies on the payroll.

03 Shared by every app

Built the careful way

  • One record for each client

    A company's, partnership's or sole trader's details are entered once, on the start page, and set up in the apps you tick. Figures flow on: the books into the accounts, the accounts into the tax computation, a sole trader's profits into their return.

  • Your data stays with you

    Everything is kept in one data file on your own computer, with payroll in its own file beside it. There is no account to create with us and nothing in the cloud.

  • Worked from the law

    Each calculation is built from UK legislation and HMRC's published guidance, with its source recorded, and checked by hand-worked examples and HMRC's own test cases where HMRC publishes them.

  • Kept up to date from the start page

    When a new version is out, the start page offers Update, or Update all. It backs up your data file first, and checks every download against a signed list.

04 Get started
