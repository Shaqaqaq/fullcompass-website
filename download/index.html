02 Installing

How to install, step by step

It takes a few minutes. You need a PC with Windows 10 or 11.

  1. Click "Download the setup"

    The button is at the top of this page. The file is saved to your Downloads folder.

  2. If your browser asks, keep the file

    Some browsers say the file "isn't commonly downloaded". Choose Keep. In Microsoft Edge that is the three dots beside the download, then Keep, then Show more, then Keep anyway.

  3. Open the file you downloaded

    Double-click it in your Downloads folder.

  4. If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears

    Click More info, then Run anyway. Windows shows this for new software it hasn't seen much of yet.

  5. Click Yes when Windows asks to allow it

    On a work PC you may need to ask whoever looks after the computer to do this step.

  6. Follow the setup

    Click Next, read the notice and click I understand. Leave all the apps ticked, click Install, and wait a minute or two.

  7. Click Finish

    Full Compass opens. Next time, find it in the Start menu by typing "Full Compass".

New to it? Once it opens, choose Help > Load Demonstration Data to fill it with made-up clients you can look around. The getting started guide takes it from there.

03 Good to know

Updates come to you

When a new version is out, Full Compass tells you when it opens. Click Update all and it does the rest. Your work is backed up first.

Your data stays on your computer

Everything you enter is kept on your own PC, not on the internet. Read the privacy note, or see Help for where it is kept and how to back it up.
