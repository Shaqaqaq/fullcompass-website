04 Corporation tax

Full Compass Business Tax

Corporation tax computations and returns, taken from the accounts, with a warning whenever the accounts change after the figures were taken.

Download the suite Next: Personal Tax →

What it does Corporation tax

  1. The computation

    Adjustments to profit, capital allowances (pools, the annual investment allowance, full expensing and first-year allowances, structures and buildings), losses and group relief.

  2. Gains and reliefs

    Chargeable gains, loans to participators, research and development and creative industry reliefs.

  3. The CT600

    With its supplementary pages, printed as HMRC's form, and the online return with the computation in iXBRL, checked against HMRC's own schemas and rules. Online filing is being tested with HMRC's test service.

  4. Sole traders and partnerships

    Self-employment pages and the partnership return, passed on to Personal Tax.

  5. Each company's tax account

    With payments, interest and quarterly instalments.

Works with Takes its figures from Accounts, and passes sole traders' and partners' figures to Personal Tax.

Full Compass is a test version: it has not been recognised or approved by HMRC or Companies House, so please don't use it to file real returns or accounts yet.
