06 Payroll

Full Compass Pay

Payroll for the practice's client companies and their directors, with each company linked to its client in the suite.

Download the suite Next: Start page →

What it does Payroll

  1. PAYE and National Insurance

    PAYE, National Insurance and student loans for 2026-27, checked against HMRC's own test data.

  2. Every pay pattern

    Weekly, fortnightly, four-weekly and monthly pay, directors' National Insurance, starters and leavers.

  3. Statutory pay

    Sick, maternity, paternity, adoption, shared parental, bereavement and neonatal care pay.

  4. Pensions

    Workplace pensions and automatic enrolment.

  5. Submissions and paperwork

    Full Payment Submissions and Employer Payment Summaries built to HMRC's specifications and checked with HMRC's own schemas; payslips, P45s, P60s, the P32 and payroll reports.

Works with Each payroll company is linked to its client on the start page, where its payroll deadlines appear.

Full Compass is a test version: it has not been recognised or approved by HMRC or Companies House, so please don't use it to file real returns or accounts yet.
