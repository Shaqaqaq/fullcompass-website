Room 03 Bookkeeping and VAT

Full Compass Books

Bookkeeping and VAT returns for the practice's clients, kept on the client's own Accounts chart, so the year-end trial balance goes straight into the accounts, line for line.

  • Test version
  • Part of the suite
Download the suite Next room: Business Tax →

In this room What it does

  1. Day-to-day bookkeeping

    Customers and suppliers, invoices, credit notes, bills, receipts and payments, bank accounts and journals.

  2. VAT returns from the books

    With cash accounting, the flat rate scheme, annual accounting, reverse charges, partial exemption and bad debt relief.

  3. Making Tax Digital for VAT

    Built, and being tested before it is used with HMRC.

  4. Reports

    Profit and loss, balance sheet, aged debtors and creditors, a cash flow planner and management packs, with exports to Excel and CSV.

  5. An audit trail that stays whole

    Posted entries are never changed or deleted: corrections are made by reversal, void or credit note.

Works with Its trial balance transfers to Accounts at the year end.

Full Compass is a test version: it has not been recognised or approved by HMRC or Companies House, so please don't use it to file real returns or accounts yet.
